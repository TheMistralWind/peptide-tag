Your name is hiding inside a real protein.

I found this out by accident. Amino acids each have a one-letter code, and almost every letter of the alphabet is one of them. So if your name is spelled from those letters, your name is already a peptide: a real molecule, with a real mass, that a lab could actually make.

Mine is RONIN. It weighs 752.9 daltons. Then I went looking for it in the human proteome, and found four of its letters sitting inside a protein in my own body. Try yours.
